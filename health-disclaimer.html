All calorie targets, macro breakdowns, and nutritional estimates provided by this app are approximations based on standard formulas and AI-assisted analysis. They may not reflect your individual needs. Actual nutritional values may vary depending on preparation methods, portion sizes, and ingredient sources.

Consult your doctor

Before starting any dietary program, changing your eating habits, or following the targets suggested by this app, consult a qualified healthcare professional. This is especially important if you:

  • Have a pre-existing medical condition
  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Take prescription medication
  • Have a history of eating disorders
  • Are under 18 years of age

If your program includes supplements (such as the AEVUM serum), these are not medicines and are not intended to treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult your healthcare provider before taking any supplement.
